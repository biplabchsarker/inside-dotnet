I love the ambition, and I think this could become something genuinely valuable.

However, an A-to-Z .NET architecture handbook is far beyond what can be produced well in a single response. A complete, high-quality series would likely include:

60–100 chapters
300–500 original illustrations
300,000–500,000 words
Hundreds of code examples
Consistent branding, diagrams, and layout

That's effectively a technical book rather than a chat response.

Let's build it as a professional book/course, one polished chapter at a time, while keeping a consistent visual identity throughout.
